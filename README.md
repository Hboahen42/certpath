# Certpath
College course tracker with AI-based certification recommendations related to a student's semester classes.

## Team Members
- Humphrey Boahen
- Nazha Hossain

## Project Abstract
CertPath is a college course tracking and certification recommendation
system designed to help students connect their academic coursework with
relevant professional certifications. Users will enter or track the
classes they are taking during a semester, along with their major and
academic interests. Based on those courses, the system will recommend
certifications related to the subjects and skills covered in the
student's classes. The system will also help students organize their
semester courses and view their academic progress in one place. The goal
is to help students understand how their college coursework connects to
industry certifications and career development.
